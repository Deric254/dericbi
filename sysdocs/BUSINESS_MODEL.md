# dericBI - Business Model Blueprint
## *How The Business Works - Complete Breakdown*

---

## 🏢 WHAT IS DERICBI

**One Line:** A data analytics service that turns messy business data into clear, profitable insights for SMEs.

**Business Type:** Service-based, founder-operated, remote-delivery

**Legal Entity:** Sole proprietorship (Deric Marangu)

**Location:** Nairobi, Kenya (serves 15+ countries remotely)

---

## 💰 HOW MONEY IS MADE

### Revenue Streams

| Stream | % of Revenue | Description |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| **Project Work** | 70% | One-time data cleanup, dashboards, analysis |
| **Training** | 20% | Teaching teams PowerBI, Excel, Python, SQL |
| **Retainers** | 10% | Ongoing monthly analytics support |

## 📈 GROWTH MODEL

