# dericBI - Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
## *The Customer Who Buys With Minimal Effort*

---

## 🎯 ONE SENTENCE

**A Kenyan SME owner losing money daily from data chaos, actively searching for help, ready to pay $20-$50 today via WhatsApp.**

---

## 📋 ICP SNAPSHOT

| Attribute | Exact Profile |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Who** | Business owner, not employee |
| **Business Age** | 1-10 years operating |
| **Employees** | 2-30 people |
| **Monthly Revenue** | KES 100K - 5M ($700 - $35,000) |
| **Location** | Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret |
| **Industry** | Retail, Wholesale, Distribution, Services |
| **Tech Level** | Uses WhatsApp, basic Excel, maybe M-Pesa statements |
| **Budget** | Has $20-$100 available for solutions |
| **Decision Speed** | Can pay within 24-48 hours |

---

## 🔥 THE TRIGGER EVENTS

**These people reach out WHEN:**

| Trigger | What Happened |
|---------|---------------|
| 💸 **Cash crisis** | End of month, no money, don't know where it went |
| 📦 **Stock shock** | Realized goods expired or aren't selling |
| 🏦 **Bank/investor request** | Asked for financial reports they can't produce |
| 📉 **Sales drop** | Revenue down, can't figure out why |
| 🧾 **Tax season** | KRA deadline, books are a mess |
| 👔 **Partner conflict** | Disputes over money, need clear records |
| 📊 **Competitor winning** | Rival business growing, they're stuck |
| 🔄 **Staff theft suspicion** | Numbers don't add up |

## ✅ QUALIFYING QUESTIONS

**To confirm they're ICP, ask:**

| Question | Good Answer | Bad Answer |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| "Do you have sales data or records?" | "Yes, in Excel/notebook/M-Pesa" | "No, nothing" |
| "What's your biggest data problem right now?" | Specific pain (stock, cash, sales) | "Just curious" |
| "When do you need this solved?" | "This week/ASAP" | "Someday" |
| "Can you invest $20-$50 to fix this?" | "Yes" | "I have no budget" |
| "Who makes the decision?" | "Me/I'm the owner" | "I'll ask my boss" |

---

## 🚫 DISQUALIFY FAST

**Not your ICP if:**

| Red Flag | Why |
|----------|-----|
| "I have no data at all" | Needs setup first (free tier) |
| "I just want to learn" | Time waster, not buyer |
| "Can you do it for free?" | No budget, no value |
| "Let me consult my partner" | Slow decision, may ghost |
| "I'm a student" | No business problem |
| "We have an IT department" | Too big, different needs |
| "Send me a proposal" | Corporate process, slow |

## 💰 PRICING PSYCHOLOGY

**Why $20-$50 works for this ICP:**

| Price Point | Psychology |
|-------------|------------|
| **$20** | "That's lunch money, I can try" |
| **$38** | "Complete solution, still affordable" |
| **$50+** | "Need to think about it" |
| **$100+** | "Let me check next month" |

**Key insight:** They lose more than $20/day to data chaos. When you frame it as *"You're losing $50/week, pay $20 to stop it"* — they buy.

## 📱 HOW THEY BUY

**The purchase journey:**

```
STEP 1: See content/ad/referral
        ↓
STEP 2: Click WhatsApp link
        ↓
STEP 3: Send first message (often just "Hi")
        ↓
STEP 4: You ask about their problem
        ↓
STEP 5: They explain pain (this is qualification)
        ↓
STEP 6: You confirm service & price ($20-$38)
        ↓
STEP 7: They send data (Excel/photos/M-Pesa)
        ↓
STEP 8: They pay via M-Pesa
        ↓
STEP 9: You deliver in 24-48 hours
        ↓
STEP 10: They refer others
```

**Total time from first message to payment: 10-30 minutes**

## 🎯 THE PERFECT FIRST MESSAGE

**When ICP contacts you, they say:**

> "Hi, I saw your page. I need help with my business data."

> "My Excel is a mess, can you help?"

> "How much do you charge for data analysis?"

> "I need a sales report urgently."

> "Can you clean my Excel file?"

**Your response framework:**

> "Hi! Yes I can help. Quick question - what's the main problem you're facing with your data right now?"

*(Let them explain = they sell themselves on needing you)*
