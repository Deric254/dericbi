## 🎯 Business Overview

**dericBI** is a data analytics and business intelligence company that empowers small-to-medium businesses, NGOs, and executives across Africa and 15+ countries to transform raw data into actionable, profitable insights.

### Core Mission
Turn confusing business data into clear, actionable decisions that directly impact profitability—within 48 hours.

### Key Differentiators
- **Speed:** 48-hour turnaround from data chaos to clarity
- **Accessibility:** Affordable pricing starting at $20/project
- **Expertise:** Multi-tool proficiency (Power BI, Python, SQL, Excel)
- **Results-Driven:** Focus on ROI and measurable business outcomes
- **AI-Powered Support:** Intelligent chatbot (DAI) for 24/7 customer assistance
