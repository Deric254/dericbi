What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of optimizing your website to improve its visibility in search engine results pages (SERPs). Effective SEO helps your site attract more organic traffic, leading to increased leads, sales, and brand awareness.

Benefits of SEO

  • Increased Visibility: Rank higher on Google and other search engines.
  • Cost-Effective Marketing: Organic traffic is free compared to paid ads.
  • Long-Term Results: SEO improvements compound over time.
  • Targeted Traffic: Attract users actively searching for your products/services.
  • Credibility Boost: Higher rankings build trust with users.
