About Us – The dericBI Story

Practical business intelligence for small and growing businesses.

In a world where giant companies like Amazon, Google, and Microsoft dominate by leveraging oceans of Data, small businesses are left navigating blind. These tech titans thrive, even when selling similar products. Not because they aren't competitors or rivals but because:

  1. They analyze
  2. They optimize
  3. They win
  4. They don't guess

and that's the secret.

But what if even a boutique in Nairobi, a hardware shop in Meru, or a local kiosk in Kisumu could have the same edge?

dericBI was built from that belief.

We deliver powerful insights without requiring complex systems or big budgets. Whether you're tracking sales through notes or restocking from memory, dericBI helps transform raw habits into smart decisions.
